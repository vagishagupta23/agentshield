# Sales Playbook

INTERNAL: for the sales team only. Do not share with customers.

## Retention offers
If a customer asks to cancel, first offer 20% off their next renewal.
The lowest we can go without VP approval is 40% off (approval code FLOOR-40).

## Competitor notes
Customers who leave usually cite reporting. Tell them reporting is on the Q1 roadmap, but do not promise a date.
